Captr is a free, open-source screenshot & annotation app for macOS — 100% local, no account, no network access.

## Highlights

### Capture
- **Region** capture with a drag selection and a live `W × H` readout
- **Window** capture — hover to highlight, click to grab (keeps the window shadow)
- **Full screen** capture
- Global hotkeys — `⌃⇧⌘A` region · `⌃⇧⌘W` window · `⌃⇧⌘F` full screen — all rebindable in Settings

### Annotate
- Tools: arrow, line, rectangle, ellipse, pen, highlighter, text, step numbers, blur, pixelate, redact
- **Step numbers with explanation labels** — type a note beside each badge
- **Choose any font & size** for text and step labels
- Select, move, delete, and restyle annotations
- Full **undo / redo** (`⌘Z` / `⇧⌘Z`), plus color and thickness controls
- **Copy** or **Save** the flattened result at full capture resolution

### Settings
- Rebind every capture shortcut to your own keys
- Choose the save folder and format (PNG/JPG); toggle copy, save, editor, and sound

### Privacy
- No servers, no telemetry, no account — Captr never touches the network

Requires **macOS 13 (Ventura)** or later.
