Captr is a free, open-source screenshot & annotation app for macOS — 100% local, no account, no network access.

## What's new in 0.3.0

### Clipboard history
- **Every image Captr copies is remembered.** Copy, Copy & Close, and the automatic copy after a capture all land in a history of your 20 most recent images.
- **Floating history panel — `⇧⌘I`.** Opens on the display under your cursor. Use the arrow keys to browse, **Return** (or double-click) to put an image back on the clipboard, **Delete** to remove it, **Esc** to close. Focus returns to the app you were in, so **⌘V** pastes right away.
- **Menu bar submenu.** *Clipboard History* in the menu bar lists recent images with thumbnails, plus *Clear History*.
- **Rebindable shortcut** under Settings → Shortcuts.
- **Private by design.** The history lives in memory only and is cleared when Captr quits. Only images copied by Captr are recorded — other apps' clipboard contents are never read.

### Editor
- **New Copy & Close button.** Copies the annotated image to the clipboard and closes the editor in one click.

Requires **macOS 13 (Ventura)** or later.
