Captr is a free, open-source screenshot & annotation app for macOS — 100% local, no account, no network access.

## What's new in 0.2.0

### Multi-monitor capture, fixed
- **Capture starts where your cursor is.** The region and window overlays now cover **every** display at once, so a capture begins on the monitor you're actually looking at — not on whichever screen happens to hold the last-focused window.
- **No more chasing the overlay.** Previously, with an editor (or any Captr window) already open, the selection would appear on that window's monitor and force you to move over to it first. It now appears under the cursor on any display.
- **Full-screen grabs the display under the cursor** instead of always capturing the primary display.
- The dimensions readout, window highlight, and Esc-to-cancel all work correctly across displays of different sizes and scales.

Requires **macOS 13 (Ventura)** or later.
